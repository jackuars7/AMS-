# Jobs to Be Done (JTBD) - Adverse Media Screening

This document articulates the core operational jobs performed by compliance professionals using the Adverse Media Screening Agent.

---

## Job 1: Resolve Screening Population (AM-01)
* **When** a new corporate or individual client is onboarded or up for periodic refresh,
* **I want to** automatically evaluate ownership and control structures against the firm's screening policy,
* **So that** all mandated UBOs, directors, controllers, and signatories are assigned traceable screening obligations without accidental omission.
* **Outcome**: 100% compliance with AML ownership thresholds; immediate identification of un-screened connected parties.

## Job 2: Consolidate Disparate Subject Information (AM-02)
* **When** evaluating a subject with data from core banking, registry filings, and corporate registers,
* **I want to** see a single consolidated profile with clear field provenance, confidence ratings, and highlighted conflicting values,
* **So that** I don't waste hours reconciling differing dates of birth, nationalities, or addresses manually.
* **Outcome**: High-trust entity resolution, zero unaddressed attribute conflicts before search dispatch.

## Job 3: Expand and Validate Subject Names and Aliases (AM-03)
* **When** screening subjects across multi-lingual jurisdictions with varied romanization systems or trading monikers,
* **I want to** leverage transliteration rules and AI suggestions with a clean four-eyes approval workflow,
* **So that** our searches capture relevant adverse findings in foreign press while preventing unapproved false-positive proliferation.
* **Outcome**: Approved alias coverage for Latin and non-Latin scripts, zero unchecked AI hallucination entering production screening.

## Job 4: Execute Deterministic, Policy-Compliant Adverse Media Searches (AM-04 & AM-05)
* **When** configuring search depth, date bounds, and language coverage,
* **I want to** enforce policy-calibrated defaults based on the client's jurisdictional risk rating,
* **So that** low-risk cases aren't bogged down in over-broad searching while high-risk cases receive required adverse category depth.
* **Outcome**: Reproducible search execution matching audited policy versions.

## Job 5: Orchestrate Resilient Multi-Source Search Gathering (AM-06)
* **When** executing search plans across news archives, court repositories, and sanctioned media registries,
* **I want to** see real-time connector coverage, circuit-breaker health, and transparent partial-success handling,
* **So that** a failure in one newspaper API doesn't crash the entire case or go silently unnoticed.
* **Outcome**: Visible coverage tracking, recoverable connector retries, and comprehensive audit defense.
